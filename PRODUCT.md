# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Prospective volunteers, food donors, chapter organizers and supporters browsing the public Better Nature website on phones and computers.

## Product Purpose
Explain the mission, help people find a chapter and enter the existing app to participate. Public charts use only public chapter records; operational records stay in the app.

## Brand Personality
Grounded, clear, alive. Modern minimal technology plus nature, as requested by the user. Preserve BN forest green, cream and blossom accents.

## Anti-references
Crowded maps, generic dashboard cards, continuous decorative animation, fake statistics, persistent pointer glows and excessive glass effects.

## Design Principles
- Clear routes into volunteering, donating and partnering.
- Real imagery and a calm interface.
- Explain data sources and uncertainty.
- Device-following light and dark appearance by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, touch targets, readable chart tables and responsive mobile layouts.
